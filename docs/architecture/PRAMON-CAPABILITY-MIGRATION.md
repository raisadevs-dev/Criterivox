# Pramon First-Class Capability Migration

Pramon owns planning and decision structure.

## Owned
- decision option construction
- trade-off structuring
- contingency planning
- decision rationale preservation
- human review preparation

## Boundary
Pramon structures alternatives. It does not choose for the human and does not execute consequential action.

The authoritative Human Residence decision pipeline delegates strategy-option construction to PramonPlanner.

Bodhex remains action-preparation/execution support. Manis remains human challenge.

## Four layers
Python: src/criterivox/Pramon/
Tests: tests/Pramon/
Dart: presentation/lib/Pramon/
Dart tests: presentation/test/Pramon/

The existing Decision & Action chamber remains authoritative; the dedicated Pramon workspace embeds it rather than duplicating it.
