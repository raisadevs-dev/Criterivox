# Criterivox S7 — Deferred Integration Backlog

S7 is closed as a sprint branch. This backlog preserves improvements identified during S7 work without pretending they are part of the completed S7 implementation.

## Character presentation

- Dynamic lip and mouth expressions rather than a permanently fixed expression.
- Eyebrow states and richer facial expression transitions.
- More refined character styling, clothing micro-detail, shadows, transparency and layered rendering.
- Hair movement and richer accessory/expert-tool presentation.
- Integration with the existing Criterivox styling system. A separate stylist character is not required.

## Analytical panels

- Replace identical generic stage panels with character-specific analytical panels and result structures.
- Keep Vivren focused on critical inspection and Tarkis focused on hypothesis exploration.

## Visualization system

- Expand the semantic visualization factory so different artifacts choose different visual forms rather than defaulting to generic graphs.
- Support charts, bar/column charts, pie/donut charts, matrices, trees, timelines and graphs where the artifact semantics justify them.
- Add internal semantic labels: axes, columns, rows, categories, nodes, branches, identifiers, values, confidence and provenance.
- Surface appropriate analytical objects directly in the rooms instead of requiring a user to open a panel first.
- Ensure Vivren and Tarkis rooms expose different analytical objects according to their roles.

## Environment and navigation

- Further reduce the navigation rail footprint and refine its collapsed/expanded behavior.
- Develop the dedicated Criterivox interior/environment system for labs, rooms, chambers and collaboration spaces.
- Give each future space its own architecture, furniture, equipment, lighting and spatial composition while preserving a coherent bureau identity.

## Cross-bureau work

- Global Intelligence Home.
- Post-S8 cross-bureau navigation and orchestration.
- Future S8 explainability/XAI functionality.

These items are intentionally recorded here so future sprints can implement them without reopening S7's completed scope or losing the decisions made during S7.
